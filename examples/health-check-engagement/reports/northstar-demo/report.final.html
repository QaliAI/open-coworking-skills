How opportunities were ranked

1) measured recurring revenue at risk, largest first; 2) measured one-time balances, largest first; 3) recurring revenue that has already left; 4) erosion of list value; 5) unquantified items ordered by severity class (data integrity, revenue leakage, churn, sales pipeline, tour conversion, speed to lead, occupancy, rooms) and then by id. Amounts are never extrapolated and never summed across different bases.

Every amount is a sum of the records listed behind it, at the basis stated (recurring at risk, one-time balance, revenue already departed, or erosion of list value). Recurring and one-time amounts are deliberately not added together, and 'erosion' figures are potential, not cash.

Engine limit
