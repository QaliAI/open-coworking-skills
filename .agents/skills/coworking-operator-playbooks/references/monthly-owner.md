# Monthly owner report

Question: "What happened this month?" or "What do we send the landlord?"

## Order

1. Confirm the month and the audience: operating owner, or landlord / management-agreement partner.
2. `coworking-monthly-owner-report`, including its audience rules.
3. Pull underlying numbers from occupancy, leakage, pipeline, speed-to-lead, and collections only for sections the audience is allowed to see.
4. `coworking-source-verifier` on the draft before it is called final.

## Minimum files

The reporting month and any exports that exist. Missing sections are omitted with a note.

## Stop rules

The landlord pack uses the same formulas and drops internal discount strategy, staff performance, and collections unless the operator confirms the agreement requires collections. Do not create a second set of occupancy numbers. Do not call the pack legal advice.
