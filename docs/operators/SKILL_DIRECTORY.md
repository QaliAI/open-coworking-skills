## Planned, not installable yet

- `coworking-cohort-retention` — Blended churn hides whether newer cohorts stick better or worse than older ones.
- `coworking-deposit-reconciliation` — Deposits held vs owed vs returned rarely reconcile after years of member turnover.
- `coworking-event-planner` — Events run on vibes - no goal, budget, or follow-through checklist per event.
- `coworking-feedback-loop` — Member feedback scatters across channels and dies unanswered.
- `coworking-win-back-campaign` — Former members are never revisited even when their cancellation reason expired.
- `coworking-proposal-builder` — Proposals are rebuilt from scratch per deal and omit terms that later cause disputes.
- `coworking-broker-channel-manager` — Broker/aggregator leads have fees and rules that make their ROI and etiquette different from direct leads.
- `coworking-waitlist-manager` — Full locations turn demand away instead of banking it against churn.
- `coworking-shift-handover` — Front-desk knowledge evaporates between shifts and locations.
- `coworking-maintenance-tracker` — Out-of-service inventory stays out of service because nobody tracks the repair pipeline against its revenue cost.
- `coworking-access-audit` — Access credentials outlive memberships - former members can still badge in.
- `coworking-listing-syndication-audit` — Marketplace and directory listings drift out of sync with real pricing and inventory.
- `coworking-review-response-drafter` — Reviews sit unanswered or get defensive replies that read worse than the complaint.
- `coworking-content-calendar` — Location marketing swings between silence and spam without a sustainable cadence.
- `coworking-mail-operations` — Mail handling for VO clients is an SLA business run on sticky notes.
- `coworking-vo-compliance-checklist` — Address-service obligations (ID verification, mail authorization forms) vary by jurisdiction and get missed.
- `coworking-cm-training-builder` — New community managers learn by osmosis; nothing is written down.
- `coworking-sales-script-coach` — Tour and phone technique varies wildly across staff with no feedback loop.
- `coworking-launch-plan` — New locations open with construction done and zero presale pipeline.
- `coworking-presale-pipeline` — Presale interest lists are not pipelines - no stages, no conversion tracking before opening day.
- `coworking-booking-flow-audit` — Tour and room-booking flows leak prospects at every extra field and dead end.
- `coworking-day-pass-upsell` — Repeat day-pass users who should be members are never offered the math.
