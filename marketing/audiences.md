# Audiences

## Primary

Coworking and flex operators: owners, GMs, community managers, and the
person who also does sales. They have exports and a question. They may
never have heard the phrase "agent skill."

Entry line for them: "I operate a coworking space. Use the OpenCoworking
skills. Ask which systems I use and what I am trying to solve. Tell me the
minimum exports. Do not ask for member emails you do not need."

## Secondary

Operators' consultants and fractional GMs who want a repeatable health
check. The open-source engine comes first. The paid review is their labor,
not a lock-in.

## Tertiary

Agent-skill authors and developers who want a vertical library with
deterministic tests, evals, and privacy rules. Product Hunt and Show HN
speak to this group. They are not the main coworking channel.

## Not the audience for a cold pitch

Vendor marketplaces, unless the note asks for a mapping correction or a
sandbox and states that we do not claim their endorsement.
