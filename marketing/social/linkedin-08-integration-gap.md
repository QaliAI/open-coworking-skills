DRAFT.

The OfficeRnD 2026 tech-stack page, from their own customers, says operators would rather connect the tools they have than replace them. 66% chose easier integration over vendor consolidation. Stripe, HubSpot, QuickBooks, Xero, and Salto show up as category leaders in that sample. Leadership of a category is not the same as a connection. Their account data says the average operator has 1.65 of five core systems connected.

I did not build another integration. I built a worksheet an agent can score: system, category, whether it is the record, what it is connected to, whether IDs are weak, whether someone retypes, whether a write is turned on. The notes column is not read, because notes are where people paste instructions to the agent.

If access control is on the list and nothing points at it, the finding is "disconnected." The skill does not turn on a badge.
