If you install five and ignore the rest for a week:

1. coworking-occupancy-revenue-dashboard — the three occupancy numbers, with the units that distort them.
2. coworking-revenue-leakage-audit — gaps between leads, deals, memberships, invoices, and inventory. It will not sum them.
3. coworking-pipeline-health — stale deals, missing next steps, duplicates. Not the answer to "why don't inquiries become tours." That is a different skill.
4. coworking-virtual-office-reconciliation — partner clients, direct clients, and invoices that do not match.
5. coworking-operator-playbooks — so you can ask the business question and let the skill pick the order.

All five are in the repo. The playbook skill is beta. The first four of this list include stable skills. Read the maturity label before you put a number in an owner email.
