# LinkedIn 4 — operator pain

DRAFT. Attribute the survey.

OfficeRnD published a 2026 flex-space tech stack landing page from a survey of 334 operators on their own platform. It is a vendor sample, not a census. They report $5.4M of combined ARR on OfficeRnD among respondents.

What they say matches the week I recognize:

- 68% name integration gaps as the top technology problem
- Across 1,276 of their accounts, the average operator has 1.65 of five core systems connected
- 14% have connected none of the five
- One account of 1,276 has all five
- The repetitive time sinks they list are invoicing and billing (27%), member communication (21%), and contracts and quotes (20%)

Buying another login does not fix a membership that is retyped into accounting. The open-source piece I can offer is a read-only map: which system is the record, which one is disconnected, which one would let an agent write. The map does not connect them. You still decide.
