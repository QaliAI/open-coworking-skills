# LinkedIn 5 — what AI gets wrong about coworking

DRAFT.

A short list of corrections I now make the agent say out loud:

1. "Full" is not one number. Offices, desks, and revenue against list price move apart when you comp a suite or discount a team.
2. A meeting room booked for two hours and cancelled is not two hours used. If the export has no status column, say so. Do not invent no-shows.
3. Notice date and end date are different fields. Someone can give notice and still be contracted through the notice period.
4. CRM "closed won" plus no membership row is a leakage finding, not a celebration.
5. Do not add "unbilled memberships" to "idle list price" to "overdue invoices" and call the sum money lost. Different bases.

If an assistant will not show the denominator, it is not ready for an owner report.
