# Positioning

## Category

Open-source operating knowledge for coworking and flexible-workspace teams.
The skills run in the agent's host. The calculations run locally on exports.

## One line

Generic AI knows business. OpenCoworking Skills teaches it coworking.

## Promise

Bring your own AI. Bring your own systems. Keep control of your data.

## What a generic assistant gets wrong

- Office occupancy, workstation occupancy, and financial occupancy are three ratios.
- Booked hours are not used hours.
- A partner-billed virtual-office client is not automatically an unbilled member.
- A notice date can differ from the agreement end date.
- A won deal in the CRM is not a membership in the workspace system.
- Leakage categories with different bases are not one total.
- Broker economics are not the same as a direct lead.

## What we do not claim

- No market benchmark inside the calculator.
- No "spaces like yours" statistic.
- No live OfficeRnD, Nexudus, Optix, Archie, or HubSpot connection. Two connectors are mock-tested prototypes.
- No vendor endorsement.
- Beta skills are not operator-proven.
- The tool does not send email, take payment, or change a contract.

## Competitor frame

Workspace systems remain the system of record. This project reads exports
and drafts the next human action. It does not replace OfficeRnD, Nexudus,
Optix, Archie, Spacebring, or Coworks.
