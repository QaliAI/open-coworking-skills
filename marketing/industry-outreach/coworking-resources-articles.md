# Coworking Resources article ideas

DRAFT pitches. Re-read their current contributor rules before sending. The article should teach. The repository gets one sentence and a link, or no link if they disallow it.

## 1. Generic AI doesn't understand coworking: ten objects to name before you trust a number

Teach company versus contact versus member versus membership versus deal. Office versus workstation versus financial occupancy. Notice versus end date. Booked versus used. Partner-billed virtual office versus direct. Do not include a tutorial install. Mention the open-source project once, as the place the definitions are written down.

## 2. Why a full coworking space can miss the rent

Explain contracted revenue versus list price, comps, and discounts, using a fictional building. No OfficeRnD survey percentage unless it is attributed and the editor wants a source. No claim that any occupancy rate is the universal break-even.

## 3. What should happen after a tour, and what the CRM usually fails to store
