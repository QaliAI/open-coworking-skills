# Vendor notes

DRAFT. One note per vendor. Do not send a blast. Do not say "we integrate with you" or "your customers use us."

Position: open-source workflows that help operators use their own exports and their own AI tools. The workspace system stays the system of record.

Ask, in this order:

1. Is the column mapping in our adapter note wrong? Please tell us what to fix.
2. Is there a sandbox or a public sample export we can fixture without customer data?
3. If your developer docs have a community page, may we link ours as an unofficial export tool, clearly unofficial?

Do not ask for a logo, a marketplace listing, or a co-marketing line.
