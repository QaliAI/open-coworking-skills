# Coworking terminology

Shared vocabulary for skills and docs. Where the industry uses one word for
several things, this glossary picks one meaning and names the alternatives so
mapping conversations with operators stay precise.

- **Operator** — the business running one or more coworking/flex locations.
- **WMS** — workspace management system (OfficeRnD, Nexudus, Optix, Archie,
  Cobot, Spacebring, etc.). Some operators call this their "coworking software".
- **Location / site / space** — one physical address. "Space" is avoided in
  data contexts because vendors also use it for rooms and units.
- **Unit** — one sellable thing: office, suite, dedicated desk, meeting room.
- **Workstation** — one seat. A 6-person office = 1 unit, 6 workstations.
- **Sellable** — currently offered for revenue. Storage rooms, staff offices,
  and comped show-suites are non-sellable and excluded from occupancy math.
- **Out of service** — temporarily unavailable (repair, renovation). Excluded
  from denominators but flagged, because "out of service" is where inventory
  goes to be forgotten.
- **Hot desk** — unassigned desk access; usually no unit assignment.
- **Dedicated desk** — an assigned desk unit.
- **Private office / team suite** — lockable unit(s) for one company.
- **Managed office** — larger custom arrangement, often enterprise.
- **Virtual office (VO)** — business address + mail (and optionally phone or
  meeting-room credits) without physical workspace.
- **Day pass / day office** — one-day products; one-time revenue.
- **Member** — a person with active access under a membership.
- **Membership / agreement / license** — the commercial contract. This model
  says "membership"; legal documents often say "license agreement" (coworking
  agreements are typically licenses, not leases — but never give legal advice).
- **Lead** — an inquiry. **Deal/opportunity** — a qualified pursuit.
  **Tour** — a scheduled visit. These are distinct records.
- **Ghosting** — a prospect who toured (or inquired) and stopped responding.
- **Notice date** — when cancellation notice was given. Distinct from
  **end date** (contractual end) and **move-out date** (physical departure).
- **Churn** — see formulas reference; always qualify as logo or revenue churn.
- **MRR** — monthly recurring revenue from memberships and recurring add-ons.
- **Comped** — complimentary (free) occupancy: staff, partners, promos.
- **Broker / aggregator / marketplace** — third parties that refer demand
  (often for a fee): flexible-office brokers, listing marketplaces.
- **VO partner** — a company that resells virtual-office service on the
  operator's address; may own billing (partner-billed) or not (direct-billed).
- **Community manager (CM)** — front-of-house operations + member experience.
- **Speed to lead** — time from inquiry to first attempted human response.
