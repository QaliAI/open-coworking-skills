# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This project serves a professional community of coworking operators and
contributors. We expect everyone participating — in issues, PRs,
discussions, and any project space — to:

- Be respectful and assume good faith
- Critique work, not people; "this formula double-counts comped units" is
  great, "you clearly don't understand coworking" is not
- Welcome newcomers and non-developers; operators who can't code are
  first-class contributors here
- Respect privacy: never post real member, client, or personnel data in
  any project space, even to illustrate a bug
- Disclose conflicts of interest when advocating for vendor-specific
  changes (working for a vendor is fine and useful; hiding it is not)

Unacceptable: harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, sexualized or
demeaning language or imagery, doxxing, sustained disruption, and
retaliation against people who report problems.

## Enforcement

Report violations privately to the maintainers (repository contact
address or GitHub's report feature). Reports are handled confidentially.
Maintainers may warn, moderate, or ban participants for violations, and
will recuse themselves from cases where they are involved.

## Scope

Applies in all project spaces and when representing the project publicly.
